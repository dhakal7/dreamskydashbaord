1. Accuracy of Information

I declare that all information and documents submitted by me are true, accurate, complete, and genuine. I understand that providing false, misleading, or fraudulent information may result in rejection of my application, cancellation of admission, visa refusal, or legal action.

2. Consent to Process Personal Data

I authorize the consultancy to collect, store, process, verify, and share my personal information and supporting documents with educational institutions, scholarship providers, government authorities, visa offices, testing agencies, financial institutions, accommodation providers, and other relevant organizations solely for the purpose of my study abroad application.

3. No Guarantee of Admission or Visa

I understand that submission of this application does not guarantee admission, scholarship, visa approval, employment, permanent residency, or any other immigration benefit. Final decisions are made solely by the respective institutions and government authorities.

I agree to pay all applicable consultancy service fees, university application fees, examination fees, visa fees, courier charges, translation charges, and other applicable expenses as communicated to me. 7. Changes by Third Parties

I understand that universities, colleges, governments, embassies, immigration authorities, and other organizations may change admission requirements, tuition fees, visa rules, scholarships, deadlines, or policies at any time without prior notice.

8. Communication Consent

10. Privacy
