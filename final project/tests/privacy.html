DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY STATEMENT

Privacy Policy

DreamSky Education Consultancy is committed to safeguarding your personal data and maintaining full privacy compliance across all educational advisory and visa guidance services.

Last Updated: February 2026
Compliance: Nepal Personal Data & MOEST Regulations
Scope: Website Visitors, Applicants & Enrolled Students

Policy Navigation

Questions regarding your personal data processing? Contact our privacy team at info@dreamsky.edu.np.
Clause 1.0

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how DreamSky Education Consultancy ("DreamSky", "we", "us", or "our") collects, stores, processes, and protects personal information obtained through our website, mobile communications, consultation sessions, and application forms. This policy applies to all prospective applicants, registered students, parents, and website visitors.

Clause 2.0

2. Information We Collect

To deliver comprehensive educational advisory services, we collect necessary personal, academic, and financial information, categorized as follows:

A. Identity & Contact Details

Full legal name, date of birth, nationality, passport numbers, email address, phone numbers, permanent residential address, and emergency contact details.

B. Academic Records & Test Results

Transcripts, marksheets, degree certificates, standardized test scores (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL, SAT, GRE, GMAT), letters of recommendation, and statements of purpose.

C. Financial & Visa Verification Documents

Sponsor income statements, bank balance certificates, tax clearance documents, relationship verification certificates, and previous visa application history required by foreign embassies.

D. Digital & Technical Data

IP addresses, browser types, device information, operating systems, referring URLs, and website interaction logs collected automatically via secure cookies.

Clause 3.0

3. How We Use Your Information

DreamSky processes collected personal information strictly for legitimate business and educational advisement purposes, including:

  • Evaluating academic eligibility and shortlisting suitable foreign universities and courses.
  • Submitting official university admission applications on your behalf.
  • Preparing student visa files, financial documentation, and interview preparations.
  • Booking official language proficiency examination slots (IELTS, PTE) and test preparation classes.
  • Communicating essential updates, offer letters, visa decisions, and tuition payment instructions.
  • Fulfilling legal reporting compliance under Ministry of Education (MOEST) guidelines in Nepal.
Clause 4.0

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade student personal data to third-party marketing companies. Information is shared only with mandatory entities directly involved in processing your education and visa applications:

1. Partner Institutions & Universities: Foreign colleges, universities, and education providers for admissions evaluation.

2. Embassy & Government Authorities: Visa processing units, immigration departments, and verification bodies.

3. Official Examination Bodies: IDP, British Council, Pearson VUE, and ETS for test result verifications.

4. Statutory Law Enforcement: Government regulators when required by binding legal court orders in Nepal.

Clause 5.0

5. Data Security & Storage Protocols

DreamSky implements multi-layered physical, technical, and organizational security controls to protect documents against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Student digital files are stored in encrypted cloud servers with restricted role-based staff access. Hardcopy records are stored in locked archives and destroyed securely after statutory retention periods expire.

Clause 6.0

6. International Data Transfers

Because study abroad applications require transmitting credentials across international borders, your personal information will be transferred to educational institutions and embassies located in target destination countries (including Australia, Canada, United Kingdom, United States, New Zealand, and European nations). By engaging our service, you consent to these international data transfers.

Clause 7.0

7. Your Data Protection Rights

As a student applicant, you maintain specific rights regarding the personal information held by DreamSky:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal documents and application records held in our file.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate academic or identity details.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of non-statutory records once application processing terminates.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for non-essential electronic communications at any time.
Clause 8.0

8. Cookies & Web Analytics

Our website utilizes standard session cookies and web analytics scripts to optimize user navigation, measure traffic patterns, and refine online user experience. You can disable cookies in your web browser settings without preventing normal access to our informative content.

Clause 9.0

9. Third-Party Web Links

Our digital pages contain links to external third-party websites (such as university portals, embassy visa portals, and test booking platforms). DreamSky holds no responsibility for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites.

Clause 10.0

10. Contact Privacy Officer & Policy Updates

DreamSky reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal or operational requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
