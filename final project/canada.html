🇨🇦 Study in Canada FAQs

Everything you need to know about studying in Canada — DLI institutions, SDS study permits, 3 year PGWP permits, GIC funding, and PR pathways.

Interactive Canada Student FAQ Desk
Select any question to expand
Q1 Why choose Canada for higher education?
Canada offers world class U15 research universities, safe multicultural cities, affordable tuition rates compared to the US, and generous 3 year Post Graduation Work Permits (PGWP).
Q2 What is the Post Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)?
The PGWP allows international graduates from Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs) to live and work in Canada for up to 3 years after completing their studies.
Q3 Is IELTS or PTE required for a Canadian study permit?
Yes. IELTS Academic (minimum 6.0 overall) and PTE Academic are accepted under SDS and non-SDS Canadian study permit applications.
Q4 Can international students work while studying in Canada?
Yes. Eligible full time students at DLIs can work off-campus up to 20 hours per week during academic terms and full time during scheduled academic breaks.
Q5 What is a Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC)?
A GIC is a Canadian bank deposit required by IRCC to prove living expense capacity when applying for a Canadian Study Permit.
Q6 What documents are needed for a Canadian Study Permit?
You need an official Letter of Acceptance (LOA) from a DLI, Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL), GIC receipt, 1st year tuition receipt, medical exam, and English test score.
Q7 How much does it cost to study in Canada?
Tuition fees range from CAD 15,000 to CAD 38,000 per year depending on college or university programs, plus GIC living expense requirements.
Q8 How does studying in Canada lead to Permanent Residency (PR)?
Canadian graduates can gain skilled work experience on their PGWP and qualify for PR through Express Entry (Canadian Experience Class) or Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP).
