🇺🇸 Study in USA FAQs

Answers to common questions about F-1 student visas, STEM OPT 36-month work extensions, Form I-20, scholarships, and US university admissions.

Q1 Why study in the United States?
The US offers unparalleled academic reputation, over 4,000 accredited universities, generous merit scholarships, state of the art research labs, and 3 year STEM OPT work extensions.
Q2 What is STEM OPT and how does it work?
STEM OPT allows graduates with eligible Science, Technology, Engineering, or Math degrees on an F-1 visa to extend their Optional Practical Training (OPT) by 24 months, for 36 total months of work authorization.
Q3 What is Form I-20 and how do I get it?
Form I-20 is an official US government document issued by a SEVP-certified school after acceptance and proof of funds. It is required to pay SEVIS fees and book an F-1 visa interview.
Q4 What tests (IELTS, TOEFL, SAT, GRE) are required for US universities?
English tests (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE/Duolingo) are required. SAT/ACT is optional at many UG universities. For PG, GRE/GMAT is required by some programs, while many offer GPA-based waivers.
Q5 Can international students work while studying in the USA?
F-1 students can work on-campus up to 20 hours per week during academic sessions and full time during breaks. Off-campus work requires CPT (Curricular Practical Training) authorization.
Q6 Are scholarships available for international students in the USA?
Yes. US universities offer merit tuition scholarships, Graduate Assistantships (GA), Teaching Assistantships (TA), and Research Assistantships (RA) that significantly reduce tuition costs.
Q7 How much does it cost to study in the US?
Annual tuition ranges from USD 15,000 to USD 55,000 depending on public or private institution type, plus living expenses estimated at USD 10,000–18,000 per year.
Q8 What is the F-1 visa interview like for Nepali students?
The F-1 visa interview at the US Embassy in Kathmandu takes 2 to 5 minutes. You must demonstrate genuine student intent, academic capability, clear career goals, and solid financial backing.
